# Note retention decisions

| Current state | Keep active when | Move or remove when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed | Work is still active, partially implemented, or awaits a decision | Reject an obsolete proposal with its reason; do not archive unshipped plans |
| Implemented | The decision still guides ownership, compatibility, persistence, security, failure behavior, or a future reintroduction | Archive only after the decision is complete and the rationale no longer guides current work but still has historical value; otherwise consolidate only when all unique rationale and inbound links are preserved |
| Rejected | The alternative remains tempting and the evidence can prevent repeating it | Remove when obsolete, superseded, implausible, or no longer useful |
| Archived | It is intentionally cited as historical evidence | Keep frozen; never present it as current authority |

On Note creation, review only same-topic records for full or partial supersession. Preserve partial supersessions and cross-link them. Do not archive or delete toward a quota, by age, or because a Note is long. Ordinary capture does not authorize an unrelated cleanup pass.
